## 📌 Project Overview
End-to-end retail analytics project analyzing 500,000+ transactions from the Online Retail II dataset. Built an interactive 4-page Power BI dashboard covering sales performance, product trends, return behavior, and business recommendations.

## 🔑 Key Findings
- 💰 Total Revenue: ₹2,04,76,260 across 43 countries
- 🇬🇧 United Kingdom dominates with ~85% of total revenue
- 📈 Q4 consistently records highest sales in both 2010 and 2011
- 🏆 A-Top products contribute ~70% of total revenue (only 14% of products)
- 🔄 8,291 orders returned with ₹16.2L revenue loss
